Our animals

Bred here, not shipped in from abroad

We breed our own rabbits, guinea pigs, mice, rats, hamsters, gerbils and reptiles. The young are sold in the shop and come either from our own store or from regular hobby breeders.

None of these animals make the journey from abroad, which often happens in dreadful conditions. The one exception is our ornamental fish: they come from reputable importers who take fish welfare seriously.
